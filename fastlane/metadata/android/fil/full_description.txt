Multilingual na YouTube Music frontend para sa Android: performance, UI/UX refinements, features, at pangmatagalang suporta.

<b>N-Zik</b> ay isang open-source fork ng <a href="https://github.com/knighthat/Kreate">Kreate</a>.

<b>Tandaan:</b> N-Zik, side project. Mas mature na alternatibo: tingnan ang <a href="https://github.com/MetrolistGroup/Metrolist">Metrolist</a>, <a href="https://github.com/vivizzz007/vivi-music">VIVI Music</a>, o <a href="https://github.com/fast4x/RiPlay">RiPlay</a>.

<b>Paggamit ng AI:</b> Gumagamit ako ng generative AI para sa code (via <a href="https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD">BMAD Method</a>), sinusuri at sinusubok bago ilabas.

Kaswal na tagapakinig man o audiophile, nagbibigay ang N-Zik ng ganap na kontrol sa musika mo nang walang kompromise sa privacy.

✨ <b>BAKIT N-ZIK?</b> Walang ads, background playback, offline caching, at customizable na UI - libre, palagi.

🎨 <b>Customizable na UI</b>
- Jetpack Compose na may Classic ViMusic mode toggle
- Mga tema: AMOLED, Deep Dark, Crisp Light + tinted accents
- 3D Carousel Player, home widgets, pagsamahang player at mini-player
- Mga artist profile, Discovery at moods, na-collapsible na home headers

🎵 <b>Karanasan sa Audio</b>
- Premium quality via YouTube Music Premium, awtomatikong inaplikar
- Synced lyrics na may editing at real-time translation
- Pagkilala ng kanta (Shazam na parang), voice search, audio visualizer
- Pro controls: speed, pitch, normalization, crossfade, volume boost, bass boost, reverb
- Audio Bar waveform seekbar para sa eksaktong navigation

💾 <b>Offline, Metadata at Privacy</b>
- Matalinong caching at downloads para sa track at playlist, na may pinipiling download quality
- FFmpeg-powered export na may buong metadata (64-bit lang)
- In-app metadata editor (AAC/Opus, cover art)
- Lokal na listening stats at history
- Auto-backup na may custom na intervals at retention

🔗 <b>Mga Integrasyon</b>
- YouTube sign-in para sa rekomendasyon at video, kasama ang eksperimental na two-way sync engine
- Last.fm scrobbling na may Now Playing at love/unlove
- MusicBrainz artist at album insights
- Discord Rich Presence, kasama ang advanced mode na may live preview
- Invidious integration para sa alternatibong YouTube streaming
- Playlist import/export: RiPlay, Spotify, YouTube Music, N-Zik

👥 <b>Magpakinig nang Sabay</b>
- Sumali sa live na pakikinig na inu-host ng ibang N-Zik user via code o invite link

🗓️ <b>Rewind</b>
- Taunang at buwang recap ng pakikinig na may auto-generated na Rewind playlists

📱 <b>Mag-play Kahit Saan:</b> Android Auto, Automotive, TV, at Tablets

🛠️ <b>Mga Feature para sa Power User</b>
- Multi-profile: hiwalay na database at settings per profile
- Auto-resume sa pagkonekta ng Bluetooth, persistent na queue, auto-fill na queue
- Smart trash, nakatagong mga awitin, dislike system, parental control
- Proxy support, network quality adaptation, log export
- Rescue Center, maintenance screen, onboarding, app shortcuts
- OTA updates mula sa GitHub, sleep timer, background playback

🛡️ 100% open-source (FOSS). Mga pangunahing pahintulot: INTERNET, RECORD_AUDIO (visualizer lamang), STORAGE (exports/backups), FOREGROUND_SERVICE, NOTIFICATIONS, REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES (OTA), BLUETOOTH, WAKE_LOCK.

📦 <b>Mga Available na Build</b>
- Full/Beta: Kumpletong build (64-bit)
- Full32/Beta32: Walang FFmpeg (32/64-bit)
- Minified: Lightweight, para sa low-end device (64-bit)
- Minified32: Lightweight na walang FFmpeg (32/64-bit)
- FOSS: Walang auto-updater, para sa F-Droid (64-bit)
- Dev/Dev32: Development builds, testing lamang

Standard build: 64-bit (FFmpeg); "32" build ay walang FFmpeg. FOSS lang ang nasa F-Droid.

Simula <b>Enero 2027</b>, hihilingin ng Google ang identity verification para sa lahat ng developer. Bilang FOSS project, tumututol ang N-Zik. <a href="https://keepandroidopen.org">Matuto pa</a> · <a href="https://www.change.org/p/stop-google-from-limiting-apk-file-usage/">Lagdaan ang petisyon</a>
